# O-Deck

## 0.1.0

First release: big tiles that work the Mac your Car Thing is plugged into.

- **Controls**: the song playing (from the Car Thing's player, or the Mac's Spotify or Music) with its cover, its
  transport and the Mac's volume as a pill you drag or turn with the wheel, beside two swipeable pages of nine:
  mute mic, Bluetooth (with what is connected), mute sound, screenshot, lock screen, Mission Control, keep awake,
  dark mode, show desktop, Wi-Fi (with its IP, and its name where macOS allows), battery, Focus (through a "Toggle
  Focus" shortcut), screen saver, quit app, brightness, empty Trash (tap twice) and sleep. The tiles that switch
  something light up while on.
- **Shortcuts**: your own shortcuts from the Mac's Shortcuts app, leaving out Apple's starter ones and untitled
  drafts, one tap to run, with search.
- **Apps**: every installed app with its own icon, grouped by category or sorted A to Z or by last opened, with
  search on an on-screen keyboard. A pressed app is edged in its icon's colour, and opening one takes you to its
  shortcuts.
- **Running**: the apps open on the Mac, the one in front in the middle, holding one for a second quitting it; tap one to bring it forward and see its keyboard shortcuts, read from its own menus as a card per menu,
  each a tap away, with Back to return.
- **Clipboard**: the text you copied lately, kept in memory on the Mac, never a password manager's, each with a
  Copy button.

Hold any tile to pin it to the head of its list. A tile answers a press in its own colour: a sheen while the Mac
works, a ring of light inside it when done, a shake when not.
