# O-Deck

## 0.1.0

First release: big tiles that work the Mac your Car Thing is plugged into.

- **Controls**: mute the mic, mute sound, volume up and down, screenshot to the Desktop, Mission Control, lock the
  screen and sleep the Mac, with the Mac's volume and mic shown above them. The wheel turns the volume.
- **Shortcuts**: every shortcut in the Mac's Shortcuts app, one tap to run.
- **Apps**: every installed app, one tap to open.
- **Pinned**: hold any tile to pin it here.
