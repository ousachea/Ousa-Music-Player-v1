# O-Phone Dock

## 0.1.1

The app's icon now shows in the store and on the Car Thing's launcher.

## 0.1.0

First release: incoming calls full-screen with answer, decline, end and mute on the presets, a wheel-scrolled feed of
your phone's notifications with their actions, and a now-playing strip.
With nothing new it shows a big clock and the date; a live call shows its caller's initials and a running timer.
