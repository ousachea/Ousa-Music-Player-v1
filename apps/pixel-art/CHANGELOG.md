# O-Pixel Art

## 0.1.0

A glowing LED wall for the dash, 32 pixels by 19, each one a soft rounded square that blooms into its
neighbours. Seven animated scenes: Fire, Plasma, Digital rain, Starfield, Rain, Life and a pixel
Clock. The wheel, a swipe or presets 1 and 3 move between them, 2 pauses, and the button under the
wheel opens them all as a gallery.

Draw your own: preset 4 or **Draw** opens the wall as a canvas with a sixteen colour palette beside
it. Drag to paint; a stroke that starts on a cell of the same colour rubs out instead. Drawings are
kept on the device and join the scenes, twinkling gently.
